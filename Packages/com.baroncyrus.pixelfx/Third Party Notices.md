Component Name: pixelfx

License Type: MIT

Copyright (c) 2026 Patch

https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx (version 0.3.1, commit 044e12a)

Every sprite sheet in `Effects` is rendered from pixelfx's effects. The effect names, descriptions, tags and hit
timings in the assets come from the same source. `Tools~/bake.mjs` adapts a few lines of pixelfx's
`tools/lib/fxlib.mjs`.

---

Component Name: Sprite Fusion pixel art VFX

License Type: MIT

Copyright (c) 2026 Hugo Duprez

https://www.spritefusion.com/pixel-art-vfx

pixelfx's simulation core and the effects that say so at the top of their source file adapt this work. Sheets
baked from those effects carry it with them.

---

Component Name: Hollow Crown

License Type: MIT

Copyright (c) 2026 rehan-remade

https://github.com/rehan-remade/hollow-crown

pixelfx's acting (exported here as each effect's `windup`, `recovery` and `hits`) adapts how Hollow Crown plays
its skills.
